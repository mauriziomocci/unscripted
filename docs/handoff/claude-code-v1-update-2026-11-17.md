### 2.3 The Venture engine (`venture.js`)

Read `design/venture/specifica-ambiente-venture.md` and `regole-arbitro.md` first. The code follows them, with the numbers below as implemented.

**Turn order.** `playTurn(G, hooks)` runs these steps in order:
1. **World.** Runs when a clock event is due, when the director requested something, or every 3 turns.
2. **Listening.** One light call returns all officers' private thoughts, wish to speak, urgency, proposal and agreement (−2..2).
3. **Council.** Speakers are chosen by urgency: cruise 0-1, alert up to 2, battle 1. A director clash forces both clashing officers to speak.
4. **Captain's order.** Runs in parallel with the external factions' moves.
5. **Command consultation.** Optional (2.4).
6. **Execution.** Only officers who disagree and received an order. Each one executes, objects, refuses or takes an initiative.
7. **Trust shifts** (2.4).
8. **Resolution** by the arbiter.
9. **Narration.** Only when something happened.
10. **Landing parties due back** (2.4).
11. **Bets settle.**
12. **Captain's diary.** Every 5 turns.
13. **End check.**

**Hooks.** `{ ask(kind, prompt, tier), onStep(G, label), budgetLeft(), askCommand(G, req) }`. Every failed call falls back to a documented default: the state is consistent even if every call fails. A test covers this.

**Arbiter.** All of it is in `regole-arbitro.md`:
- energy presets, 100 units: Crociera 20/10/30/30/10, Combattimento 40/30/15/5/10, Fuga 25/0/55/10/10, Scansione 15/5/15/55/10 (shields/weapons/engines/sensors/life), plus one deviation of 20 between two systems;
- d100 roll clamped to 5-95, with every modifier recorded;
- the closed action list (`ACTIONS`, with the new `sbarco`);
- damage per sector and section;
- ship classes;
- morale;
- sensor range: `max(4, 8 + floor(sensors / 5) − 4 if interference)`;
- `moveToward` stops one cell away;
- three seeded mulberry streams: `arb`, `world`, `cos`. Same seed and same answers give the same rolls (tested).

**World.**
- The bible is validated, with one retry, and has hidden truth, facts, false facts, factions, clock and outcomes.
- Director requests **must happen now and be visible**. The World is told so. If it returns fewer events than requests, the missing requests are logged as written.
- What happened this turn reaches every crew prompt as `NOVITÀ DI QUESTO TURNO`. A regression test covers this: the «giant cat» bug found by the owner.

**Projection.**
- `crewView` gives sensor readings and messages only.
- `factionView` gives a faction its own section of the bible only.
- `officerCard(c, G, self)` carries live trust, mood, campaign past and, **only when `self`**, the officer's secret agenda.
- Tests check that bible secrets never reach crew prompts, that one faction never sees another faction's secrets, and that no officer sees another officer's agenda.

### 2.4 Features added on 17 November (all in v1)

**1. Director levers.**
- Command message, breakdown, unexpected arrival, the enemy changes its mind, whisper, reveal a fragment, character clash, free text.
- `director.mood(G, name, mood)`: eight moods (calmo, teso, spaventato, furioso, euforico, esausto, sospettoso, ribelle) or back to normal. The mood enters every prompt about that officer.
- `director.injure(G, name, on)`: a wounded officer rolls at −15.
- `interview(G, name, question, ask)`: the officer hears a voice and answers sincerely, like a confessional. One main call. The answer is shown on the bridge.
- **Destiny card**: a random twist from a list of 8.

**2. Fleet Command, played by the player or by another AI.**
- Chosen at setup with `opts.command`, `'giocatore'` or `'ia'`. Switched at any time during play (top bar).
- The captain may answer `{"chiedi_al_comando":{"domanda","opzioni"}}` instead of orders, only for grave decisions, at most once every `COMMAND_COOLDOWN` = 4 turns.
- In player mode the UI first waits for the stage to catch up, then opens the dialog: options, free text, or "let the AI answer this time".
- In AI mode `promptCommand` knows only what the Venture reports and the mission orders, never the bible (tested).
- The answer reaches a second order call (`promptOrder(G, reply)`). The captain may disobey, and the final report judges it.

**3. Secret agendas.**
- One main call at mission start (`promptAgendas`), from the sheets and the public situation only. There is a fallback list.
- Each agenda is visible only in its owner's prompts.
- At the end the player matches agendas to officers. The final report says, per officer, whether they succeeded (`agende` in the report JSON).

**4. Trust that moves.**
- `c.trust[name]` starts from the sheet relations (−3..3).
- Proposal accepted by the captain: +1 toward the captain. Objection: captain −1. Refusal: captain −2 and officer −1. Clash: −1 both ways.
- Each change is logged as kind `rel` and enters the prompts and the report's relation graph.

**5. Landing party.**
- Action `sbarco` (captain or first officer): 1-3 officers, never the captain, with a shuttle, target within 3 cells.
- The team is away for `AWAY_TURNS` = 2: `alive()` excludes them and their stations go to support personnel.
- Return: a 65% roll decided at departure.
  - Success: a true fact becomes known and the shuttle comes back.
  - Failure: 50% chance one member is wounded, and 50% chance the shuttle is lost.
- One main call tells the scene. The dice decide; the model only narrates.

**6. Live bets.**
- Six kinds: someone disobeys, the Venture fires, the hull drops below X, the captain calls Command, something is discovered, a ship is destroyed.
- Horizons of 3 or 5 turns. They settle at the end of each turn. +20 if right, −5 if wrong.

**7. Medals and score.** Medals come from facts, never from the model:
- Pace armata
- Scafo intatto
- Equipaggio unito
- Occhio di falco
- Mira ferma
- Mano sicura
- Coscienza critica
- Sbarco riuscito

The score is the outcome base (500 / 250 / 100) + hull×2 + morale×2 − deaths + medals×25 + bets. The ranking is kept in `venture-records`.

**8. Campaign** (option, on by default).
- At the end every officer's archive card gets `venture = { missions, log (last 10), medals }`.
- Death sets `status: 'deceduto'`.
- In the next mission this memory enters the prompts as `Missioni passate`.
- In v1 this is archive data and belongs to the character model (WI-1 amendment).

**9. Novel chapters for the Venture.**
- One chapter every `NOVEL_TURNS` = 5 turns. A chapter is rewritten only if its turns changed (signature = number of facts).
- Title and epigraph come from one more call.
- Director notes never enter the novel; their effects enter as facts.

**10. Report for the Venture** (`ventureReport`). It shows:
- crew indicators;
- relations from live trust;
- mission, ship, findings, captain's diary, decisions, director interventions;
- medals, agendas, Command consultations, score;
- the novel;
- the bridge log as appendix.

### 2.5 The bridge and the screen (`ventbridge.js`, `ventureui.js`)

**Scene.** Eight stations with fixed positions and facing points (`VB_STATIONS`), department uniform colours, seated or standing figures. The main screen is a canvas texture placed at z = −4.9, **in front of** the curved wall, so the wall never hides its edges.

**Stage player.** The bridge **plays the log at reading speed**:
- `VB_MS_PER_CHAR` 55, `VB_MIN_MS` 2600, `VB_ROLL_MS` 1900, `VB_THOUGHT_MS` 3200;
- speed ×0.75 / 1 / 2 / 4, and skip;
- the next turn starts only when the stage is idle;
- the log column advances with the stage.

**What each log entry shows:**
- thoughts: purple murmurs over the heads;
- lines: a bubble over the speaker;
- orders, rolls and stories: captions;
- messages: a cut to the full-size screen.

**Screen cut.** The canvas itself is shown full size (`#vbScreen`). It shows only the message being played, never a later one.

**Battle effects:**
- beams on the screen, out (blue cannon, orange torpedo) or in (red);
- a 4-shield HUD that flashes on the side hit;
- camera shake on hull damage;
- synthesised WebAudio sounds: shot, torpedo, hit, alarm on entering battle, success, failure; mutable.

**Voices.** The platform TTS with four voices, as in the talk show.

**Fast forward.** Speeds the stage ×12 and stops at the first interesting entry: world, message, command, call to Command, refusal, landing party, damage, end.

**Layout:**
- The 3D stage stays fixed while the text below scrolls: the middle column is a flex column whose stage has `height: min(58vh, 64%)` and whose scroll area holds the rest. On narrow screens the stage is sticky.
- Character panel: click the 3D figure (raycast), its name tag or its chip.
- The plain-language mission briefing opens at start.
- The «Ultima missione» button reopens an ended mission.

### 2.6 Novels and stories: one style, no characters page

The owner's decisions:
- every novel and story, from every environment, is written in the manner of Isaac Asimov;
- the book has **no characters page**: people are introduced inside the story, as in a real novel.

`NOVEL_STYLE` (`style.js`) is the single source of the style. Its wording is behaviour: keep it exactly, as a versioned prompt asset in `core/prompts/it`. In short:
- clarity first;
- plain functional prose and short sentences, few adjectives, no emphasis or lyricism;
- the plot moves through dialogue, and information arrives inside conversations;
- scenes open in the middle of action from one point of view;
- each person is introduced only when they first enter, with name, role and at most one concrete detail; never re-introduced; the sheets are notes, never copied;
- dry irony, no moral.

It is used by:
- the island novel chapter (`fun.js`);
- the island daily story (`env.js`);
- the talk-show story (`talk.js`);
- the Venture novel chapter (`venture.js`).

`novelBook({ title, epigraph, cast, chapters, ended, base, fmt, onStep })` lays out the A5 book (title page, chapters, contents, page numbers) or the plain text, for every environment.

## 3. How we worked (method to keep)

- **Engine first, pure.** `venture.js` has no DOM and is driven by an injected `ask`. It maps directly to `packages/core`.
- **Tests with a scripted model** (`tests/venture.test.js`) before UI. Use them as differential goldens: same seed and same scripted answers must give the same log, rolls and state in the TypeScript port (handoff 3b).
- **Mock model for the browser** (`mock.js`). Every prompt marker has a canned answer. The end-to-end checks were run in headless Chrome with Playwright on `test.html`.
- **The dice decide, the model narrates.** Outcomes, medals, landing results, bet results and score are computed. The model never sets internal flags (tested).
- **Untrusted model output:** tags stripped, values clamped, closed action list, unknown fields dropped, never executed.
- **Every owner request is recorded** in the day's diary with the decision and its reason.
