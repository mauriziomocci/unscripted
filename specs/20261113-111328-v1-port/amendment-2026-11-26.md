    | message containing `model`, `not allowed`, `invalid`, `unsupported`, `unavailable` or `permission`, on Haiku | `model_refused` | Haiku off for the session; the same call resent as `main` with `lowLatency` |
    | code `rate_limited` | `rate_limited` | wait as S-08 says; at most 4 rate-limit retries, and every earlier refusal resend in the same call uses one of them (`llm.js:42, 51`) |
    | refusal string, on Sonnet or quick Sonnet | `model_refused` | no fallback; the call fails, as `llm.js:52` throws |
    | refusal string, on Haiku already switched off by a concurrent call (the race) | `model_refused` | resent as `main` with `lowLatency`, where the legacy throws; this is the assertion of the concurrency test |
    | any other error | `unavailable` | no fallback; the call fails |

## 4. FAQ

**Why rebase on the 23 November state and not keep 478d as oracle?**
The owner's decision is «deve entrare tutto», and the folder the handoff names as binding now builds page 299a. Keeping 478d would port an artifact that no longer exists and leave the novel out, which the handoff itself asks to port.

**Why pin by checksums instead of copying the folder?**
The owner forbade modifying the reference folder or copying it into the repository, except the vendoring of WI-1. A committed manifest copies no content and names every file; a local check against it before every work item catches a silent refresh. CI checks only the vendored copies, through FR-080's own manifest.

**Why Italian kind names when the owner's handoff proposes English ones?**
The literals are persisted in the saved work, and FR-011 already keeps the island's Italian literals for the same reason. The English names exist only in a proposal written before the code; the code is the oracle.

**Why not add the novel's model choice to the WI-1 contract now, like the kinds?**
The kinds are values of a field that already exists and that every client already sends. A grade field is a new axis, with precedence rules over `tier`, `lowLatency` and the settings that nobody can test before the novel exists, and its first draft here broke the spec's own naming rule. Section 2.9 is the mechanism the spec prescribes for additions a later work item needs; the WI-8 spec designs it with its consumer in front of it.

**How does the integration with the existing design hold?**
The novel reuses what earlier work items build: the style asset of WI-2, the shared queue variant of WI-7, the book layout and report model of WI-5, the archive of WI-1. WI-1 itself gains only the archive's third section, the kinds and the build changes.

**Performance and limits?**
WI-1 makes LLM calls only for the settings test. The novel's cost belongs to WI-8: 60 calls for 25 pages, about 78 with the second draft (`romanzo.js:703-710` and the 23 November addendum), sequential, so the queue's two slots are not filled by the novel alone. The legacy estimate gives 16 to 29 minutes for 25 pages (`romanzo.js:709`), about 2 to 4 calls per minute, far under the FR-031 ceiling of 120; the cost per book is that of 60 to 78 calls on the minds the player chose. The legacy page grows to 1,529,486 bytes, under the FR-084 ceiling.

**Determinism and reproducibility?**
The novel seeds from `Date.now() ^ Math.random()` (`romanzo.js:102, 430`) and dates its archive memos with `new Date()` (`romanzoui.js:238`). In core both come from the injected `Clock` and `Rng` (constitution, pure core), and the WI-8 goldens replay `tests/romanzo.test.js` with a fixed seed and a scripted model. WI-1's goldens are unaffected, except that the story prompts of the island, the talk show and the Venture now carry the 23 November `NOVEL_STYLE`.

**Why port the age clamp verbatim?**
The same parser clamp is already ported verbatim for present characters (`spec.md:224`), and fixing it for one era would be inconsistent and order-dependent. The defect is recorded and tracked for WI-6, when both forms can change together.

**Operational concerns: what if the owner refreshes the folder again?**
The local manifest check before the next work item fails, and a new amendment records the change, as A-06 now says. CI keeps checking the vendored copies, which do not change by themselves. The other failure mode this amendment introduces is the local branch of Q-26-1: work on it is backed up only on the Mac until the owner answers, so it is kept short and its commits are listed in the diary.

**Security?**
No new secret, route or field in WI-1. The real names of private persons in two reference files stay out of the public repository until the owner decides (Q-26-1). The future grade field will let a client pick a cost class, so its design in WI-8 must keep the server's allow-list and daily budget in charge; that is one reason it does not enter WI-1 untested.

**Alternatives considered.**
Porting only the 17 November afternoon state, without the quality layer: rejected, the folder no longer builds it and the addendum asks for the quality layer. Moving the novel into WI-5: rejected, WI-5 is shared reports and books for every environment, and the novel is an environment with its own engine. Making the novel part of WI-1: rejected, WI-1 is the foundation and has no game. Extra tiers instead of a grade field: rejected, grade is orthogonal to tier, whose timeouts and caps are per tier.
